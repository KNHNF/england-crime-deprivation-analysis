# 07 Results


## Rule for this file

Every finding needs at least two sentences: what the pattern is, and why it happens. The marker rewards explanation, not description. A chart with no explanation scores poorly.

## What to capture

- Each insight with its supporting chart (chart file name in `figures/`).
- The explanation for why the pattern exists. Candidate reasons to weigh: population density, differences in reporting and recording rates, policing levels, and which IMD sub-domains drive the link (income and employment often more than, say, living environment).
- The ML result and what it means: how well deprivation features separate hotspots from non-hotspots, and which features carried the most weight.
- Anything surprising or counterintuitive, flagged clearly. For example areas that are deprived but low-crime, or the reverse, are more interesting than the areas that fit the expected pattern.

## Honest framing to keep

An association between deprivation and crime is not proof that deprivation causes crime. State findings as association and prediction, not cause. Carry this into `docs/08`.

## To record here as work happens

Fill this in as charts and model results land. Do not wait until the end.
